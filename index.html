INLG 2026 · Controlled study

Token distribution versus data volume

When a balanced training mixture helps, is it the distribution of tokens across domains — or just the volume of data seen?

A controlled study across five imbalanced meeting corpora. Holding data volume fixed and varying only the allocation, we show that balancing redistributes summarisation quality across domains rather than adding it.

The core manipulation · share of a fixed token budget

Two mixtures, same budget. Balanced gives every corpus an equal share; natural follows native size — so two corpora take almost everything.

Under natural allocation, the three data-scarce domains share just 1–2% of every budget. That single fact drives most of the paper.

The confound

The setup

One model, five domains, a matched budget

Five English meeting corpora spanning more than two orders of magnitude in size, jointly fine-tuned on a single model — the imbalance that real multi-domain deployments face.

The pipeline

How the study isolates distribution

Every mixture shares the same budget, prompt, tokeniser, and seed. That shared construction is what lets a comparison at a matched budget isolate one factor at a time.

Five research questions

What the study found

Each question isolates one variable — distribution, budget, pruning, the unit of balancing, and model scale — read per domain rather than through a single aggregate.
